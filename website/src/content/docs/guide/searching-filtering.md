<p class="lead"><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> and type. Search looks at a project's <b>name</b>, your <b>notes</b> and the DAW's, its <b>markers</b>, <b>plug-ins</b>, instrumentation, <b>tags</b> and <b>custom fields</b> — and forgives typos.</p>
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/dashboard/dashboard-filters-search.png" alt="Searching the dashboard for night" loading="lazy" width="1920" height="1080"><figcaption>"night" finds <i>Midnight Dreams</i> by its name and <i>Slow Burn</i> by its notes.</figcaption></figure>
<p>The DAW, the phase and the deadline aren't searched — they have filters of their own, beside the search, and filters stack: <b>All DAWs</b>, <b>All Phases</b>, <b>Show Deadline</b>, <b>Hide Finished</b>, only projects with MIDI.</p>
