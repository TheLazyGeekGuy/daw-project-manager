<p class="lead">A short wizard sets language, theme, which tabs show, your first project folder (or a restore from Google Drive) and whether the app starts with your computer. All of it can be changed later in Settings.</p>
<figure class="shot shot-narrow"><img src="/resources/screenshots/onboarding/onboarding-welcome.png" alt="The setup wizard's first page" loading="lazy" width="840" height="394"></figure>
<p>One step picks how a <a href="/guide/project-details/">project's page</a> is laid out, with a preview of each: <b>Sections</b>, one part of the page at a time from a rail on the left, or <b>Classic</b>, everything in one long scroll.</p>
<figure class="shot shot-narrow"><img src="/resources/screenshots/onboarding/onboarding-detail-layout.png" alt="The wizard step choosing between the Classic and Sections layouts for a project's page" loading="lazy" width="880" height="568"></figure>
<div class="tip">The wizard also offers <a href="/guide/work-sessions/">Session Mode</a>, to track the time you spend on each song.</div>
