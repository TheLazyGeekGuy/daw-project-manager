<p class="lead">The riffs, chords and drum patterns in every project — Ableton Live, Cubase, Nuendo, FL Studio, Reaper — without opening the DAW.</p>
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/midi/midi-library.png" alt="The MIDI tab: every clip of every project" loading="lazy" width="1920" height="1080"></figure>
<ul>
<li>A deep scan reads them; each clip once, however often a project uses it.</li>
<li>Play any clip through the built-in synth, at any tempo, with an instrument guessed from its track.</li>
<li>Drag a clip into your DAW or a folder as a <code>.mid</code> — tempo and key included.</li>
</ul>

### Collections

<p>Your own sets of clips, in folders — copies, so they keep working whatever happens to the projects. Import <code>.mid</code> files from anywhere, name files by a scheme, export or share the whole set.</p>
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/midi/midi-collection.png" alt="A MIDI collection with folders" loading="lazy" width="1920" height="1080"></figure>

### Piano roll

<p>Click a clip's thumbnail. Edit like a DAW's key editor — select, range, eraser and pencil tools, quantize, duplicate, transpose, velocity and controller lanes — and save the result as a new clip. <kbd>?</kbd> lists every shortcut.</p>
