<p class="lead">Your library on every machine: details, to-dos, releases, MIDI, mixdowns and artwork. Your project files themselves stay where they are.</p>
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/google-drive-sync/drive-sync.png" alt="Google Drive sync and automatic sync, before signing in" loading="lazy" width="1654" height="476"></figure>
<ul>
<li>Side rail → <b>Google Drive</b> → <b>Sign in to Google Drive</b>. The app only sees its own folder in your Drive.</li>
<li><b>Sync</b> merges both ways: what you did on each machine is kept, deletions included.</li>
<li>Sync on start, or every 30 minutes to daily — even from the tray.</li>
<li>Every sync keeps the previous backup, to go back to.</li>
</ul>
