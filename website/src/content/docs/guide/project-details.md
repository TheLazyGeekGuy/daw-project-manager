<p class="lead">Double-click a project. Everything you edit saves as you type. The rail on the left shows one section at a time; the two buttons at the top left switch to <b>Classic</b>, the whole page in one scroll.</p>
<div class="shots">
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/project-detail/project-detail-metadata.png" alt="A project's page, its Project Details section open" loading="lazy" width="1920" height="1080"><figcaption><b>Project Details</b> — tempo, key, phase, deadline.</figcaption></figure>
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/project-detail/project-detail-todos.png" alt="A project's page, its TODO List section open" loading="lazy" width="1920" height="1080"><figcaption><b>TODO List</b> — what's left on this song.</figcaption></figure>
</div>
<ul>
<li><b>Details</b> — name, tempo, key (with its Camelot code), phase, deadline, notes, your own <a href="/guide/settings/">custom fields</a>.</li>
<li><b>Preview song</b> — the latest mixdown, found on its own in your export folders (Settings → Mixdown Folders) or picked by hand.</li>
<li><b>To-dos</b>, attached files and links, cover art and colour, tags.</li>
<li><b>Inside the project</b> — tracks, plug-ins and MIDI clips, after a deep scan.</li>
<li><b>Timeline</b> — every phase change, so you can see how long a song sat in mixing.</li>
</ul>
