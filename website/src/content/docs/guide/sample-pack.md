<p class="lead">Every sample your projects use, copied into one folder sorted by kind — kicks, snares, loops — with a report of which songs use what. Settings → Experimental → <b>Sample pack from your projects</b>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Each file once, however many projects copied it. Your own recordings and bounces stay out unless you ask.</li>
<li>Run it again later: only what's new is added.</li>
</ul>
