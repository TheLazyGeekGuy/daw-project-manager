<p class="lead">Every project you have, as a table (as in the <a href="/guide/overview/">overview</a>) or as cards — the same list either way. Sort by any column; choose and order the columns.</p>
<figure class="shot"><img src="/resources/screenshots/dashboard/dashboard-cards.png" alt="Projects as cards" loading="lazy" width="1920" height="1080"><figcaption>Cards: cover art, colour or icon per project.</figcaption></figure>
<ul>
<li>Row buttons: play the mixdown, open in the DAW, open the project's page, open its folder, hide it.</li>
<li>Right-click a project for everything else: move, archive, stack, delete…</li>
<li>Change the phase straight from its cell.</li>
<li>Pin projects to the bar at the top to pick up where you left off.</li>
</ul>
