<p class="lead">DAW Project Manager finds the music projects on your drives — whatever DAW made them — and keeps them in one library: what's inside each one (tempo, key, plug-ins, MIDI), where it stands, what's left to do, and the mixdown to listen to. Free and open source, on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.</p>
