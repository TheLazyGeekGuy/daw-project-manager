<p class="lead"><kbd>Cmd</kbd> on macOS where it says <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>. The full list is in the app: Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts.</p>
<table class="keys">
<tr><td><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd></td><td>Search</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd></td><td>Rescan</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd></td><td>Go to the projects table</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd></td><td>Move between projects</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>D</kbd></td><td>Open the project's page</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>P</kbd></td><td>Play / pause its mixdown</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>O</kbd></td><td>Open in its DAW</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>F</kbd></td><td>Open its folder</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd></td><td>Back (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> on macOS)</td></tr>
<tr><td><kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd></td><td>In a player: play / pause, seek 5 s (with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>: 30 s)</td></tr>
</table>
